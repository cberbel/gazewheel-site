Why these writers. Everything here is in the public domain — the authors died long enough ago that the words belong to everybody, and Homer arrives through a translation old enough to be free as well. That is also why some names are missing: Manuel Bandeira, for one, died in 1968, so his poems are still under copyright in Brazil until 2039 and are not ours to hand out. Sources for the Portuguese prose: Wikisource.

The competition is the plan. Customise your keyboard however you like, draw a passage, write it on both — the best runs win books. It starts when there are enough of us; say if you want in.
