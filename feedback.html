What has changed so far

The keyboard carries no version number — it changes when someone says it should. Newest first.

26 July 2026

  • One keyboard, two languages — English and Portuguese, same engine. The older, simpler model was retired.
  • Light background, alongside the dark one and high contrast. And colours: blue, red, green, purple, amber.
  • Ask in your own words — type “make it red”, “bigger letters” or “speak slower” in Settings and it does it.
  • Teach is now also reachable from Settings, and shows your most used words.
  • The wheel took the shape of the logo, and the arc doubles as the page indicator.

25 July 2026 — first day online

  • Published at gazewheel.org — free, in the browser, nothing to install.
  • Teach learns from files, several at once; a backup file is merged with what you already have instead of replacing it.
  • An English keyboard: keys, dictionary, word chains, voice and interface.

Before that

  • The wheel itself: the whole alphabet fixed on the outer ring, the likely letters large on the inner ring, space and delete in the middle, prediction that learns your words.
  • Then the version made for conversation: the sentence spoken the moment you close it, a chime that says wait, I'm typing, conversation mode facing the other person, a real cursor to fix a word mid-text, saved phrases, dwell selection and Teach.

What helps most

  1. A screenshot of the moment it went wrong. One image explains what a paragraph can't.
  2. Which version — English or Portuguese.
  3. Your equipment: eye tracker, head mouse, trackball, tablet; and whether dwell is on.
  4. What you were trying to write when it got in the way.

Careful with private things. Suggestions posted through the form are public, and a screenshot of the keyboard may include the sentence you were writing. Blur it, crop it, or send it by e-mail instead. Nothing you type in GazeWheel is ever sent anywhere on its own — only what you choose to attach here.
