What helps most

  1. A screenshot of the moment it went wrong. One image explains what a paragraph can't.
  2. Which version — English or Portuguese.
  3. Your equipment: eye tracker, head mouse, trackball, tablet; and whether dwell is on.
  4. What you were trying to write when it got in the way.

Careful with private things. Suggestions posted through the form are public, and a screenshot of the keyboard may include the sentence you were writing. Blur it, crop it, or send it by e-mail instead. Nothing you type in GazeWheel is ever sent anywhere on its own — only what you choose to attach here.
