# GazeWheel — what has changed

The keyboard carries no version number: it changes when someone says it should.
Newest first. (This mirrors the "What has changed so far" section on
[gazewheel.org/feedback.html](https://www.gazewheel.org/feedback.html) — keep both in step.)

## 26 July 2026

- **One keyboard, two languages.** English and Portuguese, same engine, same features. The older and
  simpler model was retired.
- **Light background**, alongside the dark one and high contrast.
- **Colours**: blue, red, green, purple, amber.
- **Ask in your own words.** Type "make it red", "bigger letters" or "speak slower" in Settings and it
  does it. Nothing leaves the computer; the change belongs to that browser only.
- **Teach** is now also reachable from Settings, and shows your most used words.
- The wheel took the shape of the logo — and the blue arc doubles as the page indicator for the inner
  ring of letters.

## 25 July 2026 — first day online

- **Published at gazewheel.org.** Free, in the browser, nothing to install.
- **Teach learns from files** — several at once. Dropping in a backup file merges it with what you
  already have instead of replacing it, so two computers can be brought together.
- **An English keyboard**: keys, dictionary, word chains, voice and interface.

## Before that — built in the Claude sessions

- **The wheel.** The whole alphabet fixed on the outer ring, so any letter can be found without
  hunting; the likely letters large on the inner ring; space, delete and swap in the middle; word
  prediction that learns your words.
- **Then the version made for conversation.** The sentence is spoken the moment you close it with a
  full stop. A chime that says *wait, I'm typing*, so the other person doesn't walk away. Conversation
  mode, with your words full-screen facing them. A real cursor, to fix a word in the middle without
  erasing everything after it. Saved phrases. Dwell selection, for eye trackers that don't click.
  And Teach, to feed it your own writing.
